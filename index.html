LeaveMate — Project Overview

Automated Enterprise AI Leave Workflow Architecture

LeaveMate is a modern work-life balance platform designed for enterprise organizations. It replaces traditional manual leave approvals with an automated multi-agent AI framework:

1. Intelligent Validation Agent:

Evaluates leave requests against company policy, checks duration limits, detects scheduling overlaps, and verifies semantic keywords with Explainable AI confidence scores.

2. Approval Recommendation Agent (Human-in-the-Loop):

Suggests Approve, Review, or Reject based on category priority weighting while keeping the ultimate decision power in the hands of authorized HR administrators.

3. Real-Time Analytics & Reporting Agent:

Calculates organizational absence rates, approval ratios, and dynamic distribution bar charts across all leave types.

4. Immutable Audit Trail:

Logs every submission, withdrawal, approval, and rejection with timestamps and user identifiers for enterprise accountability.
